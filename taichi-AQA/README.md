## ACTION QUALITY ASSESSMENT

Human action quality assessment (AQA) recently has attracted in creasing attentions in computer vision for its practical applications, such as skill training, physical rehabilitation and scoring sports events.

I believe action quality assessment is a field with practical real-world applications. Back then, human pose estimation and action recognition were booming, so I decided to bet my future on action quality assessment. 

Yet surprisingly, nearly four years have passed, and I still haven’t seen any iconic products emerge in the former two domains—let alone the latter. 

A major reason I didn’t delve deeper at the time was the scarcity of datasets, likely due to the prohibitively high cost of expert involvement. 

If we were to pursue this as an engineering project—one that allows for continuous data and label accumulation—it would be an incredibly worthwhile endeavor to explore.

## SKELETON DATA

I find skeleton data truly fascinating—it encapsulates highly concentrated key information while demanding far less computational power than image processing. 

What’s more, it can be derived from a diverse range of sources, such as imagery, laser scanners, and inertial navigation systems. 

Additionally, it lends itself well to both visualization and quantitative analysis of results. 🙈🙈
